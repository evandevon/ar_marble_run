    Set up

    1 Camera

    Point the camera straight down at the paper or whiteboard. Even light, no lamp right above.

    Make the preview match what you see when you look at the paper.

    Tip: turn on your phone's rotation lock, so the screen doesn't spin while the camera points down.

    2 Projector

    Point the projector at the whiteboard. Then:


    1. Drag it onto the projector screen and click it to fill the screen.
    2. Make sure the camera can see the whole projected picture.

    3. The projector shows a few dots; keep the camera still and stand clear for a few seconds.

    2 Corners

    Using the printed level sheet? Press Find corner marks. Otherwise click the four corners of the drawing area, in any order, and drag a dot to adjust it.
